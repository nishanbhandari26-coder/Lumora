# 🌿 Lumora — Learn. Grow. Unlock More.

**Learn today, build your tomorrow.**

Lumora is a student-focused learning platform designed for Nepal's NEB Grade 11 and Grade 12 students. It aims to make studying more engaging while encouraging students to grow beyond the classroom.

## ✨ About the Project

Lumora brings academic learning, personal development, and rewards together in one platform.

Students can work toward their academic goals, track their progress, and eventually use earned Saathi Coins to unlock personal-growth books.

## 🚀 Planned Features

- 📚 **Study Library** — NEB notes, practice questions, and past papers.
- 🤖 **AI Study Assistant** — Help with concepts and study questions.
- 🏆 **Levels & Rewards** — Earn XP and receive Saathi Coins when leveling up.
- 📖 **Book Marketplace** — Unlock personal-development books for limited periods.
- 📅 **Study Planner** — Organize study goals and activities.
- 📊 **Progress & GPA** — Track learning progress and calculate GPA.

## 🪙 Saathi Coins (SC)

Saathi Coins are virtual reward points within Lumora.

- Students earn XP through eligible learning activities.
- Reaching a new level awards Saathi Coins.
- Coins can be used to unlock eligible books.
- Book access is available for a limited period.

Saathi Coins have no cash value.
